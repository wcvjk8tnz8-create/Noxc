# User showcase

Users can publish their domains on a public gallery with an icon, title, description and link.

## How to enable

Open "Domain management → your domain → User showcase" and turn it on. **Hidden by default** — nothing appears until the owner opts in.

The public page only lists `approved` domains.

## Reward

The first time any approved domain is enabled for showcase, **+6000 points** are granted.

- Once per account: disabling/re-enabling, switching domains or adding more domains does not stack
- Disabling does not revoke
- Admins and unlimited accounts are skipped
- Accounts that already had showcase enabled before launch receive the bonus automatically on their next console visit

The amount comes from "Site settings"; setting it to `0` disables the reward and the in-page hint updates accordingly.

## Entry points

Home page, the "User showcase" item in the console sidebar, and the footer all lead to `/showcase`.
